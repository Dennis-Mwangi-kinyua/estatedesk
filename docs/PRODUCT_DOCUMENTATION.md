## Product Purpose

EstateDesk exists to provide Kenyan property businesses with a single operating system for revenue, service, and accountability.

It addresses the complexity of managing multiple buildings, tenants, leases, payments, utilities, and service requests while keeping records centralized, auditable, and accessible to role-based teams.

## Vision

EstateDesk aims to be the default operational platform for property managers, landlords, and caretakers across Kenya.

The product should feel:

- reliable and trustworthy for financial and legal workflows
- easy enough for office staff and caretakers to use day-to-day
- flexible enough to support larger portfolios and growing organizations
- compliant with Kenyan payment and utility realities

## Positioning

EstateDesk is a Kenya-ready property operations platform that connects the entire property ecosystem around financial workflows, tenant operations, and service accountability.

### One-line pitch

A modern property operations system for Kenyan landlords, caretakers, accountants, and tenants that brings rent, utilities, payments, and issues into one shared workspace.

### Differentiation

EstateDesk is differentiated by:

- built-in Kenya-specific workflows such as M-Pesa-aware payments and water billing
- caretaker-focused assignment and field update support
- strong organization-scoped tenancy with role-based visibility
- service issue and inspection workflows integrated with billing and notices

## Target Users and Personas

EstateDesk is designed for multi-user property organizations. The core personas are:

- **Property Owner / Business Owner** — needs portfolio visibility, trust in collections, and governance.
- **Organization Admin / Manager** — manages operations, staff access, reporting, and issue resolution.
- **Office Administrator** — runs tenant onboarding, lease maintenance, charges, and notices.
- **Accountant / Finance Officer** — verifies receipts, tracks balances, and handles payment allocations.
- **Caretaker / Field Operator** — submits meter readings, logs issues, and updates property condition in the field.
- **Tenant** — views bills, confirms payments, reports issues, and receives notices.

## User Roles and Responsibilities

### Admin

- Manage organization settings and access
- Approve staff and assign roles
- Monitor portfolio and financial health
- Oversee audits and sensitive actions

### Manager

- Monitor occupancy and collections
- Track unresolved issues and inspections
- Coordinate notices and follow-up actions
- Support caretakers and office staff

### Office

- Maintain tenant records and lease details
- Create charges and notices
- Manage tenant move-ins, renewals, and move-outs
- Assist with payment allocations and receipts

### Accountant

- Verify payment receipts
- Reconcile tenant balances and charges
- Review bill generation and charge accuracy
- Support financial reporting

### Caretaker

- Submit meter readings
- Log and update issues
- Support inspections and field status updates
- Execute assigned building-level tasks

### Tenant

- View bills and receipts
- Report issues or service requests
- Confirm or annotate payments
- Track notice statuses and lease details

## Core Product Pillars

### Portfolio Control

EstateDesk manages the physical portfolio and occupancy structure:

- organizations, properties, buildings, units
- building assignments and caretaker coverage
- occupancy vs vacancy tracking
- portfolio-level summaries

### Revenue and Billing

The platform supports operational revenue workflows:

- rent charges, deposits, penalties, service charges
- recurring billing and manual adjustments
- payment receipts and allocation mechanics
- arrears tracking and bill histories

### Utility and Water Management

Water billing is a first-class workflow:

- meter reading collection by assigned personnel
- usage-based bill generation
- review and approval of readings
- dispute-aware billing history

### Service and Field Operations

EstateDesk supports service accountability and field updates:

- issue tickets and repairs
- inspections and status follow-up
- notices and move-out workflows
- caretaker/field assignment and tracking

### Visibility and Accountability

Strong audit and visibility features include:

- role-based dashboards
- notification and communication support
- audit logs for sensitive actions
- organization-scoped access controls

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. Onboard a new property

- create an organization
- add a property, building, and its units
- assign staff and caretakers
- set up billing settings

### 2. Sign a new tenant and issue a lease

- create tenant profile and tenancy details
- generate lease and deposit charge
- assign move-in schedule and occupancy records

### 3. Generate rent and water bills

- create recurring rent charges
- collect and approve meter readings
- generate water bills based on usage
- review and publish bills to tenant accounts

### 4. Record payments and receipts

- log payments from M-Pesa, bank, or cash
- allocate partial payments against charges
- verify receipts and update tenant balances
- capture payment references and notes

### 5. Handle maintenance and issues

- log issues from tenants or caretakers
- assign issues to staff or caretakers
- run inspections and complete resolutions
- record status changes and closure notes

### 6. Manage move-outs and notices

- issue notices for lease expiration, arrears, or violations
- coordinate move-out inspections
- settle final balances and release deposits
- log move-out completion and unit readiness

## Competitive Advantages

EstateDesk is strongest where operational reality matters:

- built for Kenyan workflow patterns, not generic property management
- integrates financial and service operations in one system
- supports both office and field users with assigned responsibilities
- infuses strong role-based controls and auditability

## Kenyan Market Fit

EstateDesk is designed for the Kenyan real estate market with these priorities:

- M-Pesa and manual payment workflows
- caretaker / field staff participation
- utility billing and water recovery processes
- tenancy administration across small and growing portfolios
- audit-safe receipts and notices

## Key Metrics and Success Criteria

The product should be measured by:

- tenant billing accuracy and collection rate
- time to log and resolve service issues
- number of teams using a shared workspace
- frequency of reconciled payments and receipts
- reduction in spreadsheets / manual reconciliation
- user adoption across caretakers, accountants, and tenants

## Product Assumptions and Boundaries

### Assumptions

- organizations will want portfolio structure and multi-user access
- tenants expect access to their bills and payment history
- caretakers should be able to submit readings and update issues
- accountants need payment verification and allocations
- the system should support Kenyan payment conventions

### Boundaries

- EstateDesk is not a full ERP system
- it is not designed as a general CRM outside property operations
- complex accounting entries beyond payment allocations are out of scope
- property listings and marketing are secondary to operations
- utility integration beyond water and core rent workflows is optional

## Future Advancement and Roadmap

### Short-term improvements

- expand tenant mobile-friendly dashboards
- add bulk bill generation and automated reminders
- improve water bill dispute resolution workflows
- add tenant self-service receipt uploads
- add stronger platform reporting for occupancy and arrears

### Medium-term growth

- introduce payment gateway integration beyond M-Pesa
- add landlord payout and commission workflows
- support multi-currency and multi-country tenancy
- build a configurable rules engine for charge automation
- add advanced reporting and export capabilities

### Long-term vision

- provide an integrated property operations marketplace
- support API access for external accounting and property portals
- add machine-assisted portfolio insights and predictive collection workflows
- enable deeper automation for service scheduling and inspections
- build a mature SaaS platform administration layer with usage analytics
